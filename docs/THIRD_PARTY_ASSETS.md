## Source credit and distribution

The source credits above identify where the coursework obtained resources; they do not by themselves authorize publishing raw asset files. The nature pack screenshot states the [Standard Unity Asset Store EULA](https://unity.com/legal/as-terms). Aigei's current [user agreement](https://www.aigei.com/about/register), section 4.3.3, restricts redistributing downloaded works and their source files to third parties. A separate asset-specific permission would need to be checked before including those files in a public Unity project.

The supplied development document and full asset downloads are not included in this repository. No blanket permission for raw asset redistribution is claimed.
